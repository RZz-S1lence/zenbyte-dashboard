Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

This policy explains how cancellations, refunds and the legal right of withdrawal work for ZenByte Premium. It forms part of our Terms of Service. ZenByte Premium is a digital product delivered as a license key.

1. Who processes payments and refunds

ZenByte Premium is sold and processed by our authorised reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com. Paddle is the merchant of record, issues your receipt, and processes any refund. Refund requests are handled in line with this policy and Paddle's Buyer Terms. You can also contact Paddle directly via the link on your purchase receipt.

2. Cancelling a subscription

2.1. You can cancel a monthly or yearly Premium subscription at any time. After cancelling, your Premium access remains active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for, and it will not renew again.

2.2. Cancelling stops future charges. By itself, cancelling does not trigger a refund of the current period — see the refund sections below.

2.3. You can cancel from the management link in your Paddle receipt email, or by contacting us at zenbyte.support@gmail.com.

3. Your statutory right of withdrawal (EU/EEA consumers)

3.1. If you are a consumer in the EU/EEA (and in many other regions with similar laws), you normally have a 14-day right of withdrawal for online purchases, starting on the day of purchase.

3.2. Important — digital content. Premium is digital content supplied immediately. When you activate your license key (or otherwise start using Premium) within the 14-day period, you expressly request immediate performance and acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once provision has begun, to the extent permitted by law. You confirm this at checkout.

3.3. If you have not activated or used your license key, you may still withdraw within 14 days for a full refund. To do so, contact zenbyte.support@gmail.com with your order details.

4. Refunds outside the withdrawal period

4.1. Because Premium is digital and delivered instantly, purchases are generally non-refundable once the license key has been activated, except where required by law or where this policy states otherwise.

4.2. We may, at our discretion, offer a refund or partial refund in cases such as:

  • a duplicate or accidental charge;
  • Premium being substantially non-functional due to a fault on our side that we cannot resolve in a reasonable time after you report it;
  • a charge you did not authorise (please contact us before raising a chargeback).

4.3. Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Paddle. The time it takes to appear depends on your bank or card provider.

5. Lifetime purchases

5.1. The lifetime plan is a one-time purchase. The withdrawal rules in section 3 apply: once you activate your lifetime license key, the right of withdrawal is lost to the extent permitted by law, and the purchase is non-refundable except as required by law or under section 4.2.

5.2. "Lifetime" means for the lifetime of the ZenByte Service. If the Service is permanently discontinued, see section 7.

6. How to request a refund

Email zenbyte.support@gmail.com with the email address used at purchase, your order or receipt number, and the reason for the request. We aim to respond within a few business days. You may also start a request directly with Paddle using the link on your receipt.

7. If we discontinue a paid feature

If we permanently shut down ZenByte or a paid feature you are actively paying for, we will stop future billing. For active subscriptions, no further charges will be made. We may, at our discretion, provide a pro-rated refund for the unused part of a prepaid period.

8. Chargebacks and abuse

Please contact us before opening a chargeback or payment dispute — we can usually resolve issues faster directly. Fraudulent chargebacks, or abuse of refunds (for example repeatedly buying, activating and refunding), may result in suspension or permanent loss of access to the Service.

9. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy removes or limits any rights you have as a consumer under the mandatory law of your country that cannot legally be excluded. Where local law gives you stronger refund rights than this policy, those rights apply.

10. Contact

For any refund, cancellation or billing question, contact zenbyte.support@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the ZenByte Discord bot and dashboard (the "Service") collect, why we collect it, how it is used and stored, and the rights you have over it. It applies to everyone who uses ZenByte or visits the dashboard. By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for the Service is Baran Görbüz, Südoststraße, 55130 Mainz. Deutschland. ("we", "us", "our"). For any privacy question or request, contact zenbyte.support@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect only what the Service needs to work:

Discord identifiers and settings

  • Server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs and user IDs.
  • Per-server configuration and feature settings you choose.

Feature data

  • Moderation records such as warnings and case notes.
  • Leveling and activity data (XP, message/voice activity counts).
  • Ticket transcripts, where the ticket system is used.
  • Poll data, verification status and alt-detection flags.

Message content

  • ZenByte reads message content only where a feature you enabled requires it (for example text commands, leveling and logging). It does not store the content of ordinary messages, except where a feature clearly requires it, such as ticket transcripts.

Dashboard login

  • When you log in to the dashboard with Discord, we receive your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar and the list of servers you can manage. This is used to show your dashboard and is held in your session.

Premium & purchase data

  • If you buy ZenByte Premium, our payment processor Paddle handles your payment. We do not receive or store your full card details. We store your license key, the plan you bought, your Discord user ID, and limited order/customer references needed to validate and manage your subscription.

3. Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the Service — operating the bot, remembering your settings, running the features you enabled. (Performance of a contract / legitimate interest.)
  • To provide and manage Premium — activating license keys, assigning slots, validating subscriptions and handling renewals or cancellations. (Performance of a contract.)
  • To keep the Service safe — preventing abuse, spam and unauthorised access, and supporting moderation/anti-nuke features. (Legitimate interest.)
  • To comply with law — meeting tax, accounting and other legal obligations relating to purchases. (Legal obligation.)

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Who we share data with (processors)

We share data only with the providers needed to run the Service:

  • Discord — the platform the bot runs on; data flows to and from Discord by design.
  • Paddle — our Merchant of Record and payment processor for Premium. Paddle processes your payment and billing data under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting provider — the server infrastructure that runs the bot, dashboard and database.
  • Third-party content sources — where you enable social notifications, ZenByte contacts services such as YouTube, Reddit, Twitch and Kick to check for new posts or streams. Your use of those services is also subject to their own terms.

We do not otherwise share, rent or sell your data. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights, safety or security of the Service and its users.

5. How long we keep data

  • Configuration and feature data is kept for as long as ZenByte is in your server, so your settings are remembered.
  • When ZenByte is removed from a server, the data tied to that server may be deleted.
  • Premium and purchase records are kept for as long as needed to provide Premium and to meet legal and tax obligations.
  • Dashboard session data is short-lived and cleared when your session expires or you log out.

6. Your rights

Depending on your country, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted ("right to erasure");
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, or to ask what data we hold, contact zenbyte.support@gmail.com. You may also request deletion of data tied to a server you control. If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your own license key and Discord account credentials private.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required to use Discord in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

9. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including by the providers listed in section 4. Where data is transferred outside your region, we rely on appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be reflected here, and continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated Policy.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact zenbyte.support@gmail.com.
